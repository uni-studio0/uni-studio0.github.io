# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS, no build step, served by GitHub Pages from the `uni-studio0/uni-studio0.github.io` repository (site root `https://uni-studio0.github.io/`).

## Users

- Players and would-be players who saw a Uni Studio game (store listing, ad, short video) and want to know the studio and its other games, then install from Google Play.
- Store reviewers and ad networks that check the developer website (privacy policy, app-ads.txt, contact).

## Product Purpose

Studio home for Uni Studio: list every game the studio ships (currently one, Kitten Pop), send visitors to the store, and host each game's legal pages. Success: a visitor knows what Uni Studio makes within seconds and taps through to Google Play.

## Positioning

Uni Studio makes cozy, candy-bright casual puzzle games for mobile. Its first game, Kitten Pop, is a ring-spinning match puzzle: turn concentric candy rings to line up same-color kittens on a spoke and pop them. The spinning-ring mechanic is the studio's own, not a grid match-3.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive mostly on phones, from the Play listing or social video (TikTok/Shorts).
- Each game needs a stable page plus privacy policy URL that the store listing links to.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Games: Kitten Pop: Cat Match Puzzle, Android, Google Play id `com.UniStudio.KittenPop`, Puzzle / Casual, offline play, ads and in-app purchases.
- Kitten Pop facts usable on the site: hundreds of levels across candy lands; 15 collectible cat breeds (Calico, Ginger, Tuxedo, Siamese, Snow, Lilac, Golden, Gummy, Cupcake, Donut and more); outfits and hats; boosters Color Bomb, Lollipop, Undo; rainbow cats; daily gifts and spin wheel; chocolate and ice obstacles.
- Kitten Pop's existing privacy policy lives at `marwan0.github.io/kittenpop/privacy.html` (linked from Play). Data-safety form also cites `marwan0.github.io/kittenpop/delete.html`, which does not exist yet.
- Adding a game must be a copy-one-block edit.
- Undecided: whether the Play "developer website" moves to the new domain (then app-ads.txt must be copied to its root).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Uni Studio. Brand-only voice: no personal names on the site.
- Contact: game.studio.m@gmail.com.
- Logo: `assets/logo.png` (glossy candy tiles in pink, violet, mint, yellow with a pink star; "Uni" in pink, "Studio" in white, on deep plum).

## Product Principles

1. The games are the hero; the studio frame stays out of their way.
2. One tap from landing to the store.
3. Every claim is a real feature of a shipped game.
4. Scales from one game to many without redesign.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Family audience, phone-first: large tap targets, readable contrast on bright candy colors, captions on video not required (no speech), respect reduced motion.
